# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small product, engineering, operations, and creative teams use Cardboards to
organize shared work with very little setup. Members create, assign, discuss,
and move cards. Admins also manage projects, boards, people, invitations, and
settings.

## Product Purpose

Cardboards is a realtime Kanban workspace where projects contain boards and
boards contain columns and cards. It helps a team see, discuss, and move work
without turning task management into another process to manage. Success means
the board is understandable at a glance, current for everyone, and useful
within minutes.

## Brand Personality

Direct, calm, capable, and lightly opinionated. Cardboards feels like a precise
working ledger: typographic, compact, editorial, and tool-like. Its voice is
plainspoken and confident.

## Anti-references

Cardboards should not feel like a rounded, gamified SaaS dashboard, a corporate
workflow builder, or an AI-first product. Avoid nested cards, ornamental
panels, filled badge pills, gradients, glass effects, decorative motion,
multiple competing accents, and setup-heavy process language.

## Design Principles

- **Work first.** Maximize useful board area and make status legible before
  exposing secondary controls.
- **Progressive detail.** Card faces support fast scanning; richer editing
  belongs in a focused workbench.
- **Realtime without noise.** Collaboration should feel immediate, not
  animated or performative.
- **One decisive action.** Each control bar gets one clear primary action;
  everything else stays quiet.
- **Fast start.** Avoid workflow builders, lengthy onboarding, and
  configuration before value.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast on the dark palette, visible keyboard focus, semantic
names for every action, and text or icon redundancy for priority, overdue,
completed, and selected states. Toggle and filter state must not rely on color
alone. Preserve keyboard board navigation, Escape-to-close details, visible
touch controls, readable single-column mobile layouts, and reduced-motion
preferences.
